Stage 9 Amazon EKS Microservices Architecture

Decoupled Microservice Topology:

  • Frontend Service (Port 8080): Serves the Web App & SSO Client.
  • Product Service (Port 8081): Serves /api/products* via PostgreSQL & Redis.
  • Order Service (Port 8082): Serves /api/orders* and dispatches SQS events.
  • User & SSO Service (Port 8083): Handles User Registration, Authentication & Enterprise SSO.

Kubernetes Orchestration:

  • Multi-AZ EKS Managed Node Group with Instance-level TargetGroupBinding.
  • Weighted Blue/Green ALB progressive migration controller.
